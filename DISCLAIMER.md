# Responsible use

Rev Engineer and its skills are intended for analysis of software you own or are authorized to examine.

- Do not use the tools to violate license terms, terms of service, or applicable law.
- Runtime observation and process interaction follow the permissions of the launching user.
- Results are observations and inferences with explicit limitations; they are not guarantees of original source recovery.
- Keep analysis local. Do not transmit target binaries or captures to remote services unless you have explicitly chosen to do so.

Report security issues privately to the repository maintainers.
